Research

Working paper

Asset Pricing India

The Explanatory Power of the Fama–French Five-Factor Model in the Indian Stock Market

Presented at the 34th Annual Conference of the Nippon Finance Association, 2026.

I test the Fama–French five-factor model in Indian equities over 2003–2024 using a broad NSE–BSE universe. The paper documents a persistent value penalty and evaluates model performance across alternative test assets.

Draft available on request.

Research interests

Asset Pricing · Investor Behaviour · Emerging Markets · Macro-Finance

More broadly, I am interested in how institutional features and market structure shape asset-pricing patterns in emerging equity markets.
